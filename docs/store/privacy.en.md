VolumeGuard operates locally to read and adjust the master volume of output devices. It does not record audio, use the microphone or inspect played content. The app includes no accounts, advertising, analytics, app-owned telemetry or transmission to publisher servers.

The app stores a volume limit, the latest engine status and technical error logs in the local user profile. Logs may contain timestamps, Windows error messages and local file paths containing user names. They are not sent automatically. The log rotates at approximately 1 MB and retains at most one previous log alongside current settings and status.

Data remains until replaced, manually removed or removed with package data. Uninstall retention may depend on Windows and reset options. You can reset app data in Windows Settings or request support instructions. The app does not intentionally sync this data to the cloud; operating-system backup tools may handle it under their own settings.
