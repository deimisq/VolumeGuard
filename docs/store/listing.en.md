# Microsoft Store listing — English (en-US)

Status: Store-edition editorial draft, pending product-owner approval and release tests.

**Proposed product name:** VolumeGuard (reserve and verify availability).

**Summary:** Keep output volume below the limit you choose.

**Description:**

VolumeGuard helps keep the master volume of active output devices within your chosen percentage. Move the slider or click the number to enter a value from 0 to 100, save the limit and check the control status.

Minimize the panel or press X to keep the app in the notification area beside the clock. Double-click its icon to open it again. Volume control continues while the app remains running.

The Microsoft Store edition runs under your user account without requesting administrator privileges or installing a Windows service. You can change the limit or exit from the tray menu. It is not parental control or a tamper-resistant restriction. It does not start automatically when you sign in.

The limit is checked approximately every 120 ms, so brief volume peaks can occur. Windows volume percentages do not represent decibels or guarantee safe sound exposure. External amplifiers and independent hardware controls are not controlled. Compatibility depends on the audio device and driver.

Spanish and English UI follows your Windows display language. No online account, ads or app-owned telemetry. Settings and technical logs are stored locally.

**Features:**

- Master volume limit from 0 to 100 %.
- Slider or direct numeric editing, applied when you save.
- Periodic checks of active output devices, including newly detected endpoints.
- Visible status and audio/configuration issue reporting.
- Notification area icon keeps the control available with the window hidden.
- Spanish and English, local operation without external service sign-in.
